Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deliver an Oxygen Cylinder in Dhaka?

We operate round-the-clock (24/7). Inside Dhaka metropolitan area, our emergency riders deliver certified oxygen cylinders within 1 to 2 hours from booking. Our delivery includes free assembly of the regulator flowmeter and a brief tutorial for the attendants.

What is the difference between buying and renting an Oxygen Concentrator?

If the patient requires short-term supplemental oxygen (5 to 30 days during recovery from pneumonia, surgery, or COVID), renting is the most cost-effective solution. For long-term conditions (such as COPD or pulmonary fibrosis), purchasing an Owgles, Longfian, or Dynmed unit with our 2-year warranty is recommended.

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD) upon checking your items at home, bKash, Nagad, and Bank Transfer. For rental equipment, a refundable security deposit may apply based on the machine value.
